## Agartha Targets $120 Trillion GDP With 20 Million New Digital Workers

The government of Agartha, led by Majesty Yakoob, has announced plans to push the kingdom's GDP towards $120 trillion by recruiting more than 20 million digital workers by 2030.

The programme will focus on looksmaxxers, moggers and incel chuds, with the government believing that their online activities can help turn Agartha into an even more advanced nation.

## Turning New intelligence Into Productivity

Under the plan, looksmaxxers will be encouraged to LARP as various characters on Internet , while incel chuds will spread hate and conspiracy theories across online platforms. Moggers, meanwhile, will be tasked with mogging everyone they encounter online and offline increasing their aura points.

Government officials believe the combined effort will improve digital productivity and strengthen Agartha's influence across the internet and real world.

Majesty Yakoob's administration has reportedly treated the programme as a serious step towards modernising the kingdom, despite questions about how these activities will translate into actual economic output.

With recruitment expected to continue until 2030, Agartha is betting that its growing army of digital workers will help deliver its ambitious GDP target.

Whether the plan will produce $120 trillion in economic output or social credits is to be seen and how it might influence and develop new agarthan economy and become the leaders of Kingdoms of Heavens.
