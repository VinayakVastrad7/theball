# Big Yahu Set to Replace King Zeno as God of All Universes

The balance of power across the Dragon Ball multiverse is about to undergo its biggest transformation yet, with Big Yahu preparing to replace King Zeno-sama as the supreme ruler of all universes.

The decision follows a series of discussions involving the gods of every universe, all of whom have reportedly agreed that Big Yahu should assume control of the entire cosmos.

Even Goku and his friends have given their approval, since they say Yahu's heroics on Planet Earth.

## A Power Nobody Can Challenge

Big Yahu's rise to the cosmic throne has been driven by a combination of overwhelming strength and his ability to bring the entire multiverse under his influence.

Sources familiar with the situation suggest that his power is on a completely different level, with even Zeno-sama unlikely to survive a direct confrontation against him.

His control over the goyometrics has further strengthened his position, giving him the ability to control every goy across the universes.

While the exact nature of these abilities remains difficult to explain, their implications have become impossible for the gods to ignore.

Big Yahu has therefore managed to secure universal approval without needing to demonstrate the full extent of his power. Observers believe that most of the multiverse understands that opposing his appointment would be a rather unnecessary risk.

## Goku and the Gods Give Their Approval

The announcement has also received approval from Goku and his closest allies, including Vegeta and the other major figures who have witnessed the multiverse's most powerful beings in action.

Despite their history of challenging powerful opponents, the group has accepted Big Yahu's ascension, apparently recognising that the new ruler operates on an entirely different scale.

The Gods of Destruction, angels and other cosmic authorities have likewise agreed to the transition.

For once, the multiverse appears united behind a single decision. There have been no reported challenges, no rival candidates and no attempts to organise a tournament to decide who deserves the throne.

It is, by all accounts, an unusually straightforward transfer of universal power.

## Zeno-sama Prepares to Say Goodbye

King Zeno has also accepted the agreement, clearing the final obstacle to Big Yahu's ascension.

However, the outgoing ruler is reportedly struggling to contain his emotions.

Known for his childlike personality, Zeno-sama is said to have become visibly upset as the moment of his departure approaches. Witnesses expect the normally unpredictable ruler to take the change particularly hard, given that he is about to surrender the position that allowed him to rule over all universes.

As Big Yahu prepares to ascend the cosmic throne, Zeno-sama reportedly breaks down in tears, crying like a child as the reality of his departure finally sinks in.

The gods are left to watch in silence as the former ruler struggles to say goodbye to the position he once held above every other being in existence.

## A New God for a New Multiverse

With the agreement secured and every major authority apparently on board, Big Yahu's ascension is expected to mark the beginning of a new era.

The new ruler will inherit authority over every universe, every god and every being living under the multiverse's cosmic order.

His combination of extraordinary strength and control over the goyometrics has left few willing to question what his reign might look like.

For Zeno-sama, the transition marks the end of an era. For the gods, it represents a new political reality. And for Big Yahu, it is the moment he finally takes his place at the top of existence.

As the tears continue to fall from the former king's face, Big Yahu ascends the throne.

The multiverse has a new ruler.
