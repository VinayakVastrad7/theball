## A New Era of Technology

Soul Society is reportedly moving away from its traditional technology and systems as the organisation prepares to pivot towards Big Yahu tech in the upcoming quarters.

The decision comes after Captain Kurostichi approved the transition, which has already started across selected departments of Soul Society.

According to sources familiar with the matter, the organisation believes that its traditional systems are no longer capable of supporting its long term ambitions and that a fresh approach towards technology is required. Big Yahu tech has already shown what it can do in recent times in human society.

The captain has apparently decided that the best way to manage goyims, paperwork and other afterlife related activities is to introduce technology from Big Yahu's  Thel-Khaviv.

Our journalists tried to understand why an organisation that has been operating for thousands of years suddenly needed a new technology stack but nobody was available to explain it.

One employee told our crew that the old systems were working perfectly fine until management started attending technology meetings and found that goyims have gone insane and only Big Yahu's reliant tech can handle Goyims across all world.

## Captain Kurostichi Approves the Pivot

Captain Kurostichi has reportedly given his approval for the pivot and the migration of the existing technology stack has already begun.

Sources suggest that the captain has been pushing for modernisation and believes Big Yahu tech could improve efficiency across the organisation.

When asked whether the transition would affect the daily operations of Soul Society, one official said that the organisation was committed to making the process as smooth as possible.

However, several employees have reportedly expressed concerns about the upcoming changes, particularly regarding whether they would be required to learn new systems after spending centuries using the old ones.

One employee said that he had only recently learned how to use the existing system and was now being told that everything would be migrated to Big Yahu's goyim controlling tech.

He further stated that if the new system required another password reset, he would personally submit his resignation to the afterlife department.

The employee was later seen attending a mandatory technology training session.

## Big Yahu Technology to Take Over

Big Yahu is expected to play a major role in Soul Society's future technology strategy, with the organisation reportedly planning to move away from several of its traditional systems over the upcoming quarters.Big Yahu's tech has shown on how it can make total dominance over Goyims across all three world Human society , hollow world and soul society.

The full scope of the migration remains unclear, but insiders believe the transition could involve several departments and their existing infrastructure to feed Hypergamy and related topics to Goys.

Our journalists asked one of the officials whether Soul Society would also migrate its ancient records to Big Yahu servers.

The official responded that the organisation was still evaluating its options.

This has raised concerns among employees who fear that centuries of records could accidentally be deleted during the migration.

One member of the records department told our crew that losing a few documents was manageable but losing the records of every goyim  that had ever existed would create a serious administrative problem.

He also asked whether Big Yahu offered unlimited storage.

Our journalists told **Big Yahu's servers are also free for Goyims"**

Despite the concerns, the transition towards Big Yahu tech has already started, with further changes expected in the upcoming quarters.

Captain Kurostichi is understood to support the direction of the pivot as he belives he can do more experiments on goys without any problem with Thel-avivv's new radiant tech.
