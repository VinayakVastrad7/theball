## Wakanda Plans Massive Data Centres and Bot Army to Counter Agartha

Wakanda has announced plans to build massive data centres and deploy thousands of online bots as the kingdom prepares to counter Agartha in an escalating digital war.

The move comes after Agartha announced plans to recruit more than 20 million looksmaxxers, moggers and incel chuds by 2030, with Majesty Yakoob hoping to turn Agartha's new workforce into a national economic advantage.

To counter Agartha's open digital civil war , Wakanda will invest largely in Bots , build data centres and equip with large number of scripts to fight the digital war.

## The Digital Arms Race

Under the proposed programme, Wakanda will invest in large-scale data centres, automated online accounts and advanced security systems to strengthen its digital presence and protect the kingdom against Agarthan operations.

The bots are expected to counter Agartha's online influence, while the security systems will help protect Wakandan networks from digital attacks.

Officials believe that building the infrastructure to support these operations will be essential to keeping Wakanda competitive as the rivalry between the two kingdoms moves beyond the physical world.

## Prioritizing National Security

Agartha may have its army of moggers raising aura and social points across the internet, but Wakanda is preparing to answer with computing power, automated accounts and digital defence infrastructure.

The competition could soon turn into a battle of bots, with both kingdoms attempting to dominate online spaces while presenting their programmes as matters of national importance.

With Wakanda targeting 2030 for its broader development goals, the government is betting that its investment in technology and digital security will help it counter Agartha's growing influence.

The Kingdom of heavens have a long 4 years fight ahead and both are speeding up to win the war.
