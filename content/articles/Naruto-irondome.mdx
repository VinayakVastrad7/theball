## Naruto Announces 400 Billion Yen Defence Deal

Hokage Naruto has sanctioned a massive 400 billion Yen contract to build an Iron Dome system around Shinobi Village, following growing concerns about possible attacks from  Ōtsutsuki clan and various enemies on Shinobi village.

The project is expected to protect the village from incoming attacks. Several groundwork and foundation for dome has already started and full project is to be completed in span of 2 years.

The contract has been awarded to Yahu Corporation, which reportedly defeated several competitors to secure the deal. Sources close to the Hokage office said the decision was taken after Naruto reviewed multiple proposals and decided Big Yahu was the admin and the most strongest one.

Yahu corporation have already demonstrated their dome capability in war against Iran, Yahu corporations will try to complete as soon as possible given the amount of attacks the village faces.

## Yahu Corporation Gets the Contract

Yahu Corporation is expected to install advanced detection systems and intercept incoming projectiles before they can reach the village. The company has reportedly promised a modern defence network capable of identifying threats before they become a problem.

Analysts have reported that Big Yahu's dome and energy is cosmic and can defeat any kind of multiuniversal and integalactic monster or devil.

Yahu Corporation has not clarified whether its contract includes protection against giant meteors, planetary devastation, or other unexpected customer requirements.
