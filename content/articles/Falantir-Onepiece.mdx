# Imu Hires Falantir to Spy on the Five Elders as Luffy Closes In

Imu, the mysterious ruler of the world, has reportedly hired surveillance technology company Falantir to monitor the Five Elders, senior World Government officials and Monkey D. Luffy, as concerns grow over the rapidly changing balance of power.

The decision comes as Luffy and his allies continue to close the gap on Imu, raising questions about whether the ruler who has controlled the world from the shadows for centuries is beginning to feel the pressure.

According to sources familiar with the arrangement, Falantir has been tasked with collecting and analysing information across the World Government, allowing Imu to keep an eye on both his enemies and the people supposedly working for him.

The contract is believed to represent a significant expansion of the government's intelligence operations.

## Imu Has a Trust Problem

For someone who has spent centuries ruling from behind the scenes, Imu appears to have developed an unusual problem: he no longer trusts anyone.

The Five Elders, who have long served as the highest-ranking public authorities within the World Government, are reportedly among those being monitored. Senior officials are also believed to be under scrutiny, with Falantir's systems designed to identify suspicious behaviour and potential threats from within the administration.

The ruler's concerns are understandable from a strategic perspective. Luffy has repeatedly challenged established powers, gathered powerful allies and disrupted institutions and closing on to conquer and become the new king.

However, instead of relying entirely on his existing intelligence network, Imu has apparently decided that even the people responsible for protecting his position need to be watched.

## Falantir Gets Access to Everything

Falantir's reported role goes beyond keeping tabs on Luffy and the Straw Hat Pirates.

The company is expected to help Imu bring together information from across the World Government, allowing the ruler to examine connections between officials, track potential threats and eliminate any .

In theory, this could help prevent internal betrayal and improve the government's ability to respond to Luffy's growing influence.

In practice, it also means that an increasingly nervous ruler may soon have access to more information about his own administration than ever before.

Whether this will make Imu more secure or simply give him more reasons to distrust everyone remains an open question.

## The Luffy Problem

At the centre of the arrangement is Luffy himself.

The pirate has become one of the most significant threats to the existing world order, and his continued rise has made it increasingly difficult for Imu to remain comfortably hidden behind the institutions that enforce his authority.

Falantir's surveillance operations are therefore expected to place particular emphasis on Luffy, his allies and the wider network surrounding the Straw Hat Pirates.

But monitoring a pirate is one thing. Monitoring an entire government because that pirate is getting too close is another.

For Imu, the concern appears to be that the threat may no longer come exclusively from outside the system. With so many powerful individuals operating within the World Government, the ruler seems determined to ensure that nobody can make a move without his knowledge.

## The World's Most Watched Government

The Falantir contract marks a potentially significant shift in how Imu intends to protect his rule.

Rather than relying solely on the authority of the Five Elders and the strength of the World Government, he appears to be building a surveillance system capable of monitoring the people responsible for maintaining that authority , he also has drawn quite an inspiration from Yahu systems.

Luffy is still out there, getting closer and Imu wants to eliminate him and his lineage as soon as possible.
